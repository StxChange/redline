COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you send the cars—or rather, don’t.

Do I mail you my cars?

No. The service starts with photographs. Keep the collection safely in your possession.

Are the prices guaranteed sale prices?

Ranges are informed opinions based on visible condition and comparable market activity. Final sale prices depend on authenticity, venue, timing and demand.

What if a car cannot be identified from my photo?

The report marks the uncertainty and requests a specific follow-up view instead of forcing an identification.

Can you help me sell the collection?

Selling help can be discussed separately after the independent evaluation. Any sales arrangement is separate from the appraisal price.
