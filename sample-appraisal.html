HOW TO TIGHTEN THE RANGE

Five photos can turn this into a defensible inventory.

  1. Full front and backOne straight-on image of each card, including corners.
  2. Every blister edgeClose-ups that reveal lifting, glue, cracks or repairs.
  3. Base stampCountry, casting marks, rivets and matching patina.
  4. Car detailsInterior, glass, wheels, engines, wings and small parts.
  5. ProvenancePurchase history, prior owner and any grading records.
Important valuation rule

A reseal, reproduction part or mismatched button can move a car from the carded tier toward loose-car value. Do not clean, polish or open anything before authentication.
